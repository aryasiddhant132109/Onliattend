About OnliAttend
Developed as an NTCC (Non-Teaching Credit Course) Project. Fully client-side biometric facial recognition using deep convolutional neural networks (face-api.js on TensorFlow.js). No external face data is uploaded, ensuring 100% student biometric privacy.
